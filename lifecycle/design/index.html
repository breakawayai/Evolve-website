The approach

Buildable, by design.

Five habits that keep the drawings honest to the field.

  1. 01

    Fix the basis first

    Requirements, assumptions, responsibilities and acceptance criteria confirmed before drawing.

  2. 02

    Coordinate every interface

    How each system affects adjacent disciplines, procurement, construction and operation.

  3. 03

    Test constructability on paper

    Field execution, access, sequencing and modular fabrication resolved before mobilization.

  4. 04

    Control change, do not absorb it

    Decisions documented and weighed against the basis, not passed around informally.

  5. 05

    Design for commissioning and maintenance

    Acceptance, documentation, access and support designed in.

Direct answers

Direct answers.

What makes data-center design different from ordinary building design?

Mission-critical electrical, thermal, controls and equipment systems have to run as one. An unclear interface in an office is an inconvenience; in a data center it reaches operations.

When does constructability enter the design?

While decisions can still move. The people who will build it check the design against field conditions, access, sequencing and package interfaces.

Why consider maintenance during design?

Because Maintain starts in Design. Access, isolation, documentation and interfaces decide how the facility is maintained, and cost little to change on paper.
